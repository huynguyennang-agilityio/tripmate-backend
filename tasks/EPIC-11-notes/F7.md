# F7 Notes -- Review Round 2

The notes of diff review round 2 for card F7, Idempotency keys
([development-flow.md](https://github.com/huynguyennang-agilityio/tripmate-docs/blob/main/docs/development-flow.md), section 2.4).
Round 2 found one defect, R2-1: the start-up order check read only a route's `dependencies=[...]`, so a capability taken as a handler parameter, or `idempotent` declared as a parameter, was not reported; it is fixed in `a39df98`. The rows R1-1, R1-8, and R1-12 are round 1 findings the product owner left to later cards. The Decision column stays empty until EPIC-11 closes.
